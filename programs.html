What We Do

Our programs focus on real communication, confidence building, and supportive peer learning.

English Conversation Practice

We provide regular speaking practice to help students use English more naturally in real-life communication.

Confidence Building

We encourage students to speak up, make mistakes, and improve through practice in a positive environment.

Interactive Activities

Our sessions may include discussion topics, games, short speeches, Q&A practice, and presentation activities.

Volunteer Service

Club members contribute their time to support younger students and promote meaningful English learning.

Our Speaking Levels

Students build their speaking skills step by step, from confidence to clear and impactful expression.

Level 1

Foundation

Goal: Build confidence to speak

Students practise daily conversation, pronunciation, basic sentence patterns, and school-life scenarios. This level helps learners overcome speaking hesitation and become more comfortable using English.

Level 2

Structured Speaking

Goal: Speak clearly

Students learn how to organize ideas through opinion speaking, storytelling, topic discussions, and speaking frameworks such as PREP and PEEL. This level helps students move toward clear, paragraph-level expression.

Level 3

Advanced Output

Goal: Speak with impact

Students practise presentations, debates, mock interviews, impromptu speaking, and persuasive communication. This level helps students strengthen logic, confidence, and real-world expression.

How Our Sessions Work

Our sessions follow a simple and supportive process to help students learn at the right level.

1

Pre-assessment

We learn about the student’s speaking level, goals, and learning needs.

2

Level Placement

Students are placed into a suitable level based on confidence and speaking ability.

3

Tutor Matching

Students are matched with a volunteer tutor or session leader.

4

Speaking Session

Students practise English through conversation, activities, and guided speaking tasks.

5

Feedback

Students receive supportive feedback to help them improve step by step.

Future Signature Programs

As E-Talk Club grows, we plan to develop more specialized speaking programs.

Monthly Speaking Circle

A group discussion program where students apply speaking skills in real conversations, improve fluency, and practise expressing ideas with confidence.

IELTS Speaking Practice

A structured practice program for students preparing for IELTS Speaking, including Part 1, Part 2, Part 3 simulations and feedback on fluency, vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation.

TED-style Talk Project

A presentation project where students prepare and deliver English talks that can showcase long-term speaking growth and personal expression.

E-TALK CLUB

A student-led volunteer organization helping younger students build English speaking confidence through conversation and peer support.
