What We Offer

E-Talk Club focuses on practical speaking opportunities. Our sessions are designed to help students speak more often, feel less nervous, and become more comfortable using English.

Conversation Practice

Students practise everyday English through simple conversations, questions, and discussion topics. The focus is on speaking more naturally and becoming more comfortable.

Confidence Building

We encourage students to speak without worrying too much about mistakes. Our goal is to help students feel more confident when expressing themselves in English.

Peer Learning

Students learn in a friendly peer-supported environment. Student volunteers help guide the conversation and make the session feel relaxed.

Speaking Activities

Sessions may include short introductions, role-play, topic questions, storytelling, and small discussion activities depending on the student’s comfort level.

E-TALK CLUB

A student-led non-profit club helping students build English speaking confidence through conversation, peer support, and friendly practice.
