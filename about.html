About Us

E-Talk Club is a student-led non-profit club built around peer support, English speaking practice, and a friendly learning environment.

Our Background

E-Talk Club was created by students who understand how difficult it can be to speak English confidently. Many students want to improve their speaking skills, but they may not always have a comfortable space to practise.

Our club gives students a chance to talk, listen, ask questions, and learn from peers. Instead of making English practice feel like a formal class, we try to make it friendly, natural, and supportive.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help students become more confident English speakers through low-pressure conversation practice and peer encouragement. We believe students can grow faster when they feel comfortable trying, making mistakes, and learning together.

Student Volunteer Tutors

Peer Support from Student Volunteers

E-Talk Club sessions are supported by student volunteers. Our volunteers help lead conversations, encourage participation, and make English practice feel less stressful. The goal is not to create a formal classroom, but to build a friendly peer-learning space where students can practise speaking more often.
