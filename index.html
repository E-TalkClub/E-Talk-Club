Why We Started E-Talk Club

Many students study English for years, but still feel nervous when they need to speak in real conversations. They may know vocabulary and grammar, but they do not always have enough chances to practise expressing their ideas naturally.

E-Talk Club was created to give students a low-pressure place to practise English speaking. Through peer conversations and simple guided activities, we help students become more comfortable, confident, and willing to speak.

What We Do

We create friendly speaking opportunities led by students, for students.
